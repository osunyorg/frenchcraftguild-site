Textile design and meta-craft

body: >-


Atelier Marion Chopineau is an innovative and interdisciplinary independent studio. With 20 years’ experience in craftsmanship, it is firmly committed to a sculptural approach to textile design. The team specialises in three-dimensional sculpted pieces, seamless knits and macramé bags, and relishes the challenges posed by complex lattice structures woven directly onto the mannequin, three-dimensional relief embroidery and unique moulded textiles. By putting its expertise and curiosity at the service of haute couture and luxury ready-to-wear designers, the workshop has developed its own distinctive style through the reinterpretation of techniques such as basketry, net-making, mosaics, hairdressing, sculpture and more.

Atelier Marion Chopineau is an innovative cross-disciplinary indépendant workshop. Its 20 years old craftsmanship very much moves towards a sculptural approach of textile design. The team is skilled in volume shaped pieces, seamless netting and macrame bags and enjoy challenges of complex lattice structures woven directly on the dummy, tree dimensional relief embroidery and never seenmold-casted textiles.

The workshop Marion Chopineau is independent textile creation workshop. For 14 years, she has put her know-how and curiosity at the service of fashion designers and luxury ready-to-wear. For them it develops embroidery and textile manipulations resulting from the offset and reappropriation of techniques such as basketry, net manufacturing, mosaic, hairdressing, sculpture, etc.

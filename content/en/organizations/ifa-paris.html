Present in Paris and Istanbul, IFA Paris trains future fashion professionals from the creation of a collection to its marketing.

The school offers two complementary courses, Stylism & Creation and Fashion Management & Marketing which combine courses, practical projects and professional experience.

Training

  • Bachelor Stylism & Modelism · 3 years
    Learn how to draw a silhouette, build a pattern and create a garment, until you create a collection that expresses your creative identity.
  • Bachelor Marketing Fashion · 3 years
    Understand consumer trends and behaviours, then learn to position a brand and develop its marketing strategy.
  • MBA Cycle Specialized · 2 years
    After a first year of common core, students choose their second year specialization according to their field.

- Direction Artiste Mode-Design de Mode Durable-Entrepreneuriat Mode & Design-Communication & Media Mode-Marketing Mode et Luxe-Management Mode Durable-Fashion Tech & Entrepreneurship

  • Short courses in English
    Get started in fashion or deepen a practice, from stylism to upcycling, according to the chosen program.

novinov develops innovative bio-based materials and products in France for use in 3D printing and plastics processing

body: >-


In a world in search of ecological renewal, novinov is at the crossroads of scientific research, environmental transition and artistic experimentation. novifil, a fully bio-based and biodegradable 3D printing filament, combines a plant-based PLA with Miscanthus fibres grown near Fontainebleau, without artificial irrigation or chemical inputs. It offers a singular material, both technical and sensitive, to the subtly organic texture and to the golden hue revealing fine shades under the light. novifil invites artists, designers and artisans to explore an eco-responsible aesthetic, where precision, durability and material poetry meet in objects that bear the memory of the plant and the territory.

In a world in search of ecological renewal, novinov is at the crossroads of scientific research, environmental transition and artistic experimentation. novifil, a fully bio-based and biodegradable 3D printing filament, combines a plant-based PLA with Miscanthus fibres grown near Fontainebleau, without artificial irrigation or chemical inputs.

It offers a singular material, both technical and sensitive, to the subtly organic texture and to the golden hue revealing fine shades under the light.

novifil invites artists, designers and artisans to explore an eco-responsible aesthetic, where precision, durability and material poetry meet in objects that bear the memory of the plant and the territory.
